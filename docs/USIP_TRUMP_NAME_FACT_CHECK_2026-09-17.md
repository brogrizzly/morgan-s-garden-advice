# United States Institute of Peace and the Donald J. Trump name

Research checkpoint: 17 September 2026. Public reading note for Morgan's Garden Advice.

## Is it real?

**Yes: the Trump branding is real.** The live USIP website displays “Donald J. Trump Institute of Peace.” This directly verifies the website's presentation; it does not authenticate every image circulating online or prove the institution's promotional claims. [USIP website, accessed 17 September 2026](https://www.usip.org/).

Associated Press reported on **3 December 2025** that the State Department announced the renaming and Trump's name appeared on the headquarters building. The report includes a dated AP photograph credited to Matthew Lee. There is therefore evidence beyond an AI-generated screenshot or social-media caption. [AP report, republished by Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/news/2025/12/04/trump-is-fighting-the-institute-of-peace-in-court--now--his-name-is-on-the-building).

## Congressional origin versus public branding

The official **2024 U.S. Code**, Title 22, section 4603(a), states:

> There is hereby established the United States Institute of Peace.

The source credit dates that provision to **19 October 1984**, Public Law 98-525, title XVII, section 1704. That is the congressional foundation. The 2024 text names the United States Institute of Peace; a website heading or building sign alone is not evidence that Congress amended that text. [Official GPO statutory text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title22/html/USCODE-2024-title22-chap56-sec4603.htm).

Source discrepancy: the AP republication linked above says Reagan signed the bill in 1985. For the establishment provision's date, these notes use the official statutory source credit, **1984**.

## Why the legal question is separate

The litigation challenged the administration's removal of USIP leadership and takeover of the institution. The University of Michigan's Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse records a **19 May 2025** district-court judgment against the takeover, followed by a **27 June 2025** appellate stay. A stay pauses enforcement; it is not itself a final merits resolution. The summary also records a September 2025 abeyance and November 2025 refusal to partially vacate the stay. [Case history and linked court filings](https://clearinghouse.net/case/46245/).

**Current legal outcome remains unverified in this note.** Although the archive carries a recent update stamp, its narrative still discusses late-2025 proceedings. That stamp must not be treated as proof that the narrative resolves the case as of September 2026. This check did not establish a final appellate disposition or locate an enacted congressional renaming law.

## What the evidence supports

- **Verified:** the Trump name appears on the live institutional website.
- **Independently reported:** the December 2025 announcement and physical signage.
- **Historically established:** Congress created USIP through the 1984 legislation identified above.
- **Not established here:** a congressional statutory renaming, final judicial approval, or whether any particular unprovided image was AI-generated.

For historical reading links, see [Title 22 and congressional history](TITLE_22_CONGRESSIONAL_HISTORY_READING.md). Political claims on the branded website are claims by its publisher, not conclusions adopted by these notes.
