## Chicks / brooder record

The flock began with **22 chicks**. One chick died today; **21 remain**. The cause is not established by the photographs or current observations and is therefore recorded as unknown.

The surviving chicks were photographed sleeping together in a close communal cluster beneath and near the red heat lamp. The blue plastic-tote brooder contains wood-shaving bedding, a chick waterer, and a shallow feeder. No chick appears isolated from the sleeping group.

The photographs do not establish the brooder's exact temperature. Their distribution should be checked again when they are awake: a comfortable group should move freely between the warmed area and cooler space. Persistent tight piling beneath the lamp while awake can suggest cold; crowding far from it or panting can suggest excessive heat.

## Seed collection

Mature **calendula seeds** were collected by hand after the flower heads were left on the plants to dry. The normal mixture of curled, crescent-shaped, and tightly coiled ribbed achenes was present. Their dry tan-to-brown appearance is consistent with mature collected seed, although germination remains the real viability test.

This batch belongs to the garden's locally selected calendula line: plants that completed flowering and seed production under current Coos Bay garden conditions. Keep the seeds dry with airflow until no residual moisture remains, then store them with the species, collection date, and—if remembered—the parent flower's color or location.
