# G4LO Creative Influences

G4LO is a software and systems project, but its creative lineage is not limited to software. Music—especially politically conscious hip-hop and the work surrounding Rage Against the Machine—has materially influenced its language, posture, and insistence that systems should expose rather than conceal power.

## KRS-One and Boogie Down Productions

[KRS-One's work](https://www.krs-one.com/about) is a major influence on G4LO's treatment of knowledge as something practiced, taught, questioned, and carried into public life. The influence is not a borrowed slogan or aesthetic costume. It is the harder expectation that an intelligent system should help people reason, learn, and retain authority over their own decisions.

## Zack de la Rocha and Rage Against the Machine

[Rage Against the Machine](https://www.ratm.com/)—especially Zack de la Rocha's writing and performance—has influenced G4LO's attention to institutional power, historical memory, resistance, and the difference between a system's stated purpose and what it actually does to people. Their work is a reminder that technical language can never make power neutral.

[Official Rage Against the Machine videos](https://www.youtube.com/user/RATMVEVO)

## Tom Morello

[Tom Morello's work](https://www.tommorello.com/) is a major influence on G4LO's idea that familiar tools can be used in unfamiliar, inspectable ways. His approach to the guitar—treating constraints, noise, switches, feedback, and physical technique as a larger instrument—maps naturally to G4LO's interest in small components, visible state, unconventional interfaces, and human-controlled systems.

## A living lineage

KRS-One, Zack de la Rocha, Tom Morello, and Rage Against the Machine are the first explicitly recorded musical influences, not the entire lineage. Other rappers and musicians will be added when they are named and linked carefully rather than folded into a vague or invented list.
