# G4LO Creative Influences

G4LO is a software and systems project, but its creative lineage is not limited to software. Music—especially politically conscious hip-hop and the work surrounding Rage Against the Machine—has materially influenced its language, posture, and insistence that systems should expose rather than conceal power.

This is not a sterile bibliography. **KRS-One, Zack de la Rocha, Rage Against the Machine, and Tom Morello are direct inspirations for G4LO. I fucking love these artists and their work.** Their music has kept me thinking, questioning, building, cleaning, gardening, and refusing to accept that a system deserves obedience merely because somebody put it in charge.

## KRS-One and Boogie Down Productions

[KRS-One's work](https://www.krs-one.com/about) is a direct inspiration for G4LO's treatment of knowledge as something practiced, taught, questioned, and carried into public life. The inspiration is not a borrowed slogan or aesthetic costume. It is the harder expectation that an intelligent system should help people reason, learn, and retain authority over their own decisions.

## Zack de la Rocha and Rage Against the Machine

[Rage Against the Machine](https://www.ratm.com/)—especially Zack de la Rocha's writing and performance—is a direct inspiration for G4LO's attention to institutional power, historical memory, resistance, and the difference between a system's stated purpose and what it actually does to people. Their work is a reminder that technical language can never make power neutral.

[Official Rage Against the Machine videos](https://www.youtube.com/user/RATMVEVO)

## Tom Morello

[Tom Morello's work](https://www.tommorello.com/) is a direct inspiration for G4LO's idea that familiar tools can be used in unfamiliar, inspectable ways. His approach to the guitar—treating constraints, noise, switches, feedback, and physical technique as a larger instrument—maps naturally to G4LO's interest in small components, visible state, unconventional interfaces, and human-controlled systems.

## A living lineage

Music broadly is a direct inspiration for G4LO: far too many bands, rappers, musicians, songs, and records to list without turning this page into a record store. KRS-One, Zack de la Rocha, Tom Morello, and Rage Against the Machine are especially important named examples, not the entire lineage. More may be added when a particular connection is worth explaining, but absence from this short list does not mean absence from the project's creative history.

## Love, credit, and honesty

These links send people toward the artists' own work because that work matters to me. KRS-One, Boogie Down Productions, Zack de la Rocha, Tom Morello, and Rage Against the Machine are major creative influences on G4LO. They are not formally affiliated with the project; I am simply giving loud, honest credit to people whose work I love.
