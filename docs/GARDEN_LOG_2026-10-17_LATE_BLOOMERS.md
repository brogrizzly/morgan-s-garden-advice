## Garden reflection — Thích Nhất Hạnh

> “When you plant lettuce, if it does not grow well, you don’t blame the lettuce.”

— Thích Nhất Hạnh, *Peace Is Every Step*, excerpt reproduced by Still Water Mindfulness Practice Center in [“Don’t Blame the Lettuce”](https://www.stillwatermpc.org/dharma-topics/don_t_blame_the_lettuce/) (7 May 2012). Plum Village also preserves his teaching in [“Remember Your Insights. Live Them. Don’t Blame the Lettuce”](https://web.plumvillage.app/item/remember-your-insights-live-them-dont-blame-the-lettuce-en-subtitles).

The passage was recalled as being about cabbage; the sourced wording here uses lettuce. His broader teaching extends understanding and care to people as well as plants.

For this garden: look at light, water, temperature, soil and time before assigning blame. The calendulas carry fresh flowers, developing seeds and aging leaves together. Record those conditions and changes with patience.
